Diabetes (Sugar) Patients: 6 Things to Keep in Mind

Sugar is controlled not only by medicine but by daily habits. Small steps make a big difference.

What to do

  • Take the medicine prescribed by your doctor on time. Do not stop or reduce it without asking.
  • Cut down sugar, refined flour and sweet drinks.
  • Walk briskly for 30 minutes every day.
  • Get your sugar tested regularly and keep a record.
  • Check your feet daily. Do not ignore blisters or wounds.
  • Record fasting and after-meal readings separately.
⚠ When to see a doctor: Excess thirst or urination, dizziness, sweating and shaking (low sugar), blurred vision, or wounds that do not heal: meet a doctor soon.
